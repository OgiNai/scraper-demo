THE “SPARK 3L” Jacket

 Highly technical and packable, Spark are our ultralight-waterproof jacket. Good water resistant and strong windprotections makes it ideal for trowing on in a storm. This 3-layer material is fully windproof and waterproof, highly robust and has a high degree of breathability, coated with advanced DWR (durable water repellent), the jacket protects you where needed. Featuring largе hood that fits over the helmet, reflective 3M Scotchlite band help you stand out in low light.

FABRICS & FEATURES

The seams are completely sealed with a special tape to prevent moisture ingress.

Ultralight, high-performance, 100% waterproof and windproof 3-layer laminate with excellent breathability.The outer fabric has a very smooth, dull surface. The back of the laminate has a thin mesh that protects the membrane. 

The higher the water column rating of a material, the greater the water pressure it can withstand and the greater the level of waterproofness.

This garment provides optimal moisture transport. The extremely breathable fabric guarantee optimal body climate. The MVT rate of this garment is not less than 20.000g/m²/24h.

Large hood with built-in elastic for quick fit

The zipper is laminated with a water-repellent PU-layer that prevents water and moisture build up VISLON® AquaGuard® / YKK

 

Completely PTFE-free and PFC-free
